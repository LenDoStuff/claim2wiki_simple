# Project Purpose

Edit this file before importing PDFs. It guides analysis, page selection, merging,
and review suggestions. Be specific about what you want to learn.

## Goal

Build a connected, evidence-based reference from the supplied documents. Explain
the important subjects and how findings from different sources relate.

## Key Questions

1. What are the main findings, and what evidence supports them?
2. Where do sources agree, disagree, or apply to different situations?
3. What limitations and open questions matter for using this knowledge?

## Scope

**In scope:** Central entities, concepts, methods, findings, comparisons, and
syntheses supported by these PDFs. Preserve technical definitions and exact data.

**Out of scope:** Unsupported claims, incidental mentions with no relevance to the
goal, and conclusions that go beyond the evidence.

## Thesis

No thesis assumed yet. Develop tentative conclusions from the evidence and keep
contradictions, uncertainty, and competing explanations visible.
